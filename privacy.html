Privacy Policy — Odd Atlas Uploader

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Odd Atlas Uploader ("the app") is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Odd Atlas YouTube channel. The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

After the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel: basic channel information, the channel's own videos and their statistics (views, likes, comments, YouTube Analytics reports), and comments on the channel's own videos.

How the data is used

Only to upload the channel's own videos and thumbnails, reply to comments on those videos, and measure which videos perform best so future videos can be improved. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the channel owner's own computer/server. Statistics older than 30 days are deleted automatically. Comment text is kept only as long as needed to avoid replying twice.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions; after that the app can no longer access any data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the owner through the Odd Atlas YouTube channel.
